# A little space to think.

Welcome to **Marknote**. A quiet, lightweight space for writing in Markdown.

Write in the editor and see your words take shape in the preview. Start with an idea, a note, or a new story.

> Writing helps bring a little clarity to your thoughts.

## Start here

- Focus on your content with **Markdown**
- Jump between sections using the outline
- Choose **Edit**, **Split**, or **Preview** in the toolbar button group
- Press `⌘ S` to save your words on your Mac

## Turn ideas into words

Keep your hands on the keyboard and your mind on the page. Search commands with `⇧⌘P`, or use `⇧⌘O` to switch documents.

| Action | Shortcut |
| :--- | :--- |
| Bold selected text | ⌘ B |
| Insert a link | ⌘ K |
| Find in your document | ⌘ F |
| Toggle focus mode | ⌘ ⇧ F |

Paste a screenshot to insert an image beside your saved document. Select words and paste a URL to make a link. Use Tab to move through table cells, or `⌥⌘T` to align a table.

For a longer draft, try **Paragraph Focus** and **Typewriter Scrolling** in the View menu.
